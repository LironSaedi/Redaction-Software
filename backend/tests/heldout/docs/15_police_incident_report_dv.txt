On [[DATE|08/16/2025]] at approximately 2314 hours, Ofc. [[PERSON|Doyle]] #[[LAW_ENFORCEMENT_ID|3390]] and I were dispatched to
[[ADDRESS|7420 Callaghan Rd Apt 12B]] ([[INDIRECT_LOCATION|Pinecrest Apartments]]) in reference to a disturbance /
possible family violence. Dispatch advised the caller, a neighbor, heard a female screaming
"stop, I can't breathe" and a child crying, and that the screaming had stopped.

We arrived at 2321 hours. I could hear a male voice yelling from inside 12B. The neighbor, later
identified as W1 [[PERSON|Rosalind Pham]], was standing on the breezeway outside her door at 12C and
pointed at 12B. I knocked and announced "San Antonio Police." A [[DEMOGRAPHIC|white male]], later identified as
S1 [[PERSON|Dariusz Kowalczyk]], opened the door. S1 was sweating, breathing heavily, and had a strong odor
of an alcoholic beverage on his breath. S1 stated "everything's fine, she's being dramatic." I
observed a [[DEMOGRAPHIC|white female]], later identified as V1 [[PERSON|Grace Hill]], sitting on the kitchen floor
holding the left side of her neck and crying. S1 was detained in handcuffs for officer safety
and placed in the rear of my patrol unit (Unit 6232) by Ofc. [[PERSON|Doyle]].

V1 stated the following in summary (BWC recorded): She and S1 have lived together for about four
years and have a three-year-old daughter, [[PERSON MINOR|Lily]]. S1 came home at approximately 2245 after
drinking at "[[INDIRECT_LOCATION|the icehouse on Bandera by the Valero]]." S1 accused her of texting a coworker. S1
grabbed her phone off the counter and she tried to take it back. S1 threw the phone against the
floor, cracking it. V1 told S1 she was calling the police and went to the bedroom to get her
iPad. S1 followed her, pushed her into the hallway wall, and placed both hands around her neck
and squeezed. V1 stated she could not breathe or yell, that "everything went dark around the
edges" and she thought she was going to die. V1 stated she believed this lasted "ten or fifteen
seconds." She scratched at S1's hands and he let go when [[PERSON MINOR|Lily]] began crying in the bedroom. V1
fell and struck her left elbow on the floor. V1 stated she urinated on herself during the
strangulation. V1 stated S1 then said "if you call them I swear to God I'll finish it" and "they'll
deport me and it'll be your fault."

I observed red linear marks on both sides of V1's neck, consistent with fingers, a reddened area
under her chin, and petechiae (small red dots) in the white of her left eye. Her voice was hoarse.
She complained of pain when swallowing. There was a fresh abrasion on her left elbow. Her shorts
were wet. I photographed the injuries. ACS EMS Medic 31 evaluated V1 on scene at 2338; V1 refused
transport stating she would go once her daughter was picked up. Medic 31 crew advised V1 of the
risks of delayed symptoms from strangulation. V1 signed refusal.
