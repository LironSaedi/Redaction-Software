INVESTIGATOR'S NARRATIVE OPINION OF WHAT HAPPENED
  Unit 1 (tractor-semitrailer) was traveling westbound on E Ben White Blvd main lanes approaching
  S Congress Ave. Unit 2 was northbound on S Congress Ave in the left turn lane, turning left
  (west) onto Ben White Blvd on a protected green arrow. Unit 1 failed to stop for the steady red
  signal for westbound traffic and struck Unit 2 on the left (driver) side in the intersection.
  Unit 2 was pushed approx. 60 ft west and came to rest against the center median. Unit 1 came to
  rest approx. 140 ft west of the area of impact.

  Signal timing obtained from City of Austin (event log for signal #418): WB phase red for 6.8 s
  at time of impact. W1 dashcam video (time-stamped 17:38:21) shows Unit 2 NB-to-WB arrow green and
  Unit 1 entering the intersection after the WB signal displayed red. Unit 1's ECM/ELD data
  requested from carrier (preservation letter sent [[DATE|03/17/2025]]). Unit 1 driver stated "the light was
  yellow, I couldn't stop the load in time." Unit 1 driver stated he was talking to dispatch on a
  hands-free headset; driver's phone was observed in a dash mount. Driver declined consent to
  search phone.

  Unit 2 driver was extricated by AFD and transported with serious injuries. Unit 2 child passenger
  was transported with minor injuries and was in a properly installed booster seat.
