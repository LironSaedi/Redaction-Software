             CRIMINAL COMPLAINT AND AFFIDAVIT FOR PROBABLE CAUSE (WARRANTLESS ARREST)

BEFORE ME, the undersigned authority, on this day personally appeared the affiant, who, after
being by me duly sworn, on oath deposes and says:

1.  My name is [[PERSON|Javier Castañeda]]. I am a peace officer licensed by the Texas Commission on Law
    Enforcement (TCOLE PID [[ID_NUMBER|0718244]]) and employed by the San Antonio Police Department, badge
    #[[LAW_ENFORCEMENT_ID|4127]], assigned to the West Patrol Division. I have been a peace officer for 9 years and
    have received training in the investigation of family violence and non-fatal strangulation.

2.  I have good reason to believe and do believe that on or about the [[DATE|16th day of August, 2025]],
    in [[LOCATION?|Bexar County]], Texas, [[PERSON|DARIUSZ PIOTR KOWALCZYK]], hereinafter called "Defendant," did then
    and there intentionally, knowingly, or recklessly cause bodily injury to [[PERSON|GRACE ELIZABETH HILL]],
    a member of the Defendant's household and a person with whom the Defendant has or has had a
    dating relationship, as described by Tex. Fam. Code §§ 71.0021(b), 71.005 and 71.003, by
    intentionally, knowingly, or recklessly impeding the normal breathing or circulation of the
    blood of [[PERSON|GRACE ELIZABETH HILL]] by applying pressure to her throat or neck with his hands,

    AGAINST THE PEACE AND DIGNITY OF THE STATE. Offense: Assault -- Family Violence -- Impeding
    Breath or Circulation, Tex. Penal Code § 22.01(b)(2)(B), a felony of the third degree.

3.  My belief is based on the following facts and information:

    a.  On [[DATE|August 16, 2025]], at 11:09 p.m., the Bexar Metro 911 Network received a call from
        [[PERSON|Rosalind Pham]], who reported hearing a woman screaming "stop, I can't breathe" through the
        shared wall of her apartment at [[INDIRECT_LOCATION|Pinecrest Apartments]], [[ADDRESS|7420 Callaghan Road]], San
        Antonio, [[LOCATION?|Bexar County]], Texas.

    b.  I arrived at 11:21 p.m. with Officer [[PERSON|Megan Doyle]] (#[[LAW_ENFORCEMENT_ID|3390]]). Defendant answered the
        door of [[INDIRECT_LOCATION|Apartment 12B]]. I observed the complainant, [[PERSON|Grace Hill]], seated on the kitchen
        floor holding her neck and crying.

    c.  The complainant stated that she and the Defendant have lived together for approximately
        four years and share a child, [[PERSON MINOR|L.R.K.]], age [[AGE|3]]. She stated the Defendant came home
        intoxicated, accused her of infidelity, broke her phone, pushed her against a wall, and
        squeezed her neck with both hands for approximately 10 to 15 seconds, during which she
        could not breathe, her vision narrowed, and she lost control of her bladder. She stated the
        Defendant released her only when the child began crying.

    d.  I observed red linear marks on both sides of the complainant's neck consistent with
        fingers, redness beneath her jaw, petechial hemorrhage in the sclera of her left eye, and a
        hoarse voice. She reported pain when swallowing. I photographed these injuries.

    e.  I observed fresh scratches on the backs of both of the Defendant's hands. The complainant
        stated she scratched the Defendant's hands while trying to pull them away from her neck.

    f.  The Defendant, after Miranda warnings, denied choking the complainant and stated he "just
        held her off." The Defendant had a strong odor of alcoholic beverage and admitted to
        drinking "four or five" beers.

    g.  [[PERSON|Rosalind Pham]] provided a written statement consistent with the complainant's account.

    h.  A search of Texas Crime Information Center records reflects that the Defendant was
        [[CRIMINAL_HISTORY|previously convicted of Assault Causes Bodily Injury -- Family Member on May 11, 2019,
        in County Court at Law No. 9 of Bexar County, Texas, Cause No. 576114]].

    i.  Based on the foregoing, I arrested the Defendant without a warrant at 12:22 a.m. on
        [[DATE|August 17, 2025]], pursuant to Tex. Code Crim. Proc. art. 14.03(a)(4).

4.  The affiant requests that the Defendant be held to answer and that bail be set in an amount
    sufficient to protect the complainant, consistent with Tex. Code Crim. Proc. arts. 17.15 and
    17.152, and that a Magistrate's Order for Emergency Protection be issued under art. 17.292
    designating [[PERSON|GRACE ELIZABETH HILL]] and [[PERSON MINOR|L.R.K.]] as protected persons. See also Ex parte Gomez,
    624 S.W.3d 573 (Tex. Crim. App. 2021) (bail considerations).
