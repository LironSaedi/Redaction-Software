1.  My name is [[PERSON|Yesenia Marisol Alvarado Cruz]]. I was born on [[DATE_OF_BIRTH|May 27, 1997]], in [[LOCATION?|El Progreso]], Yoro,
    Honduras. My Honduran national identity card (DNI) number is [[NATIONAL_ID|0501-1997-04418]]. My Honduran passport

    I speak Spanish and some Garifuna. This declaration was read back to me in Spanish by the interpreter.

2.  I entered the United States on or about [[DATE|June 14, 2019]], by crossing the Rio Grande near [[LOCATION?|Hidalgo]], Texas,
    with my son [[PERSON MINOR|Kevin]], who was then one year old. We turned ourselves in to Border Patrol. We were held for
    four days and released with a Notice to Appear. [[IMMIGRATION_STATUS|I have no lawful immigration status in the United
    States. I filed my asylum application on May 30, 2020, within one year of my arrival]] (receipt
    [[CASE_NUMBER|ZHN2004418827]]). [[IMMIGRATION_STATUS|My Employment Authorization Document (category C08) is valid until 11/02/2026]];
    the card number is [[ID_NUMBER|SRC2190418821]].

    MY LIFE IN HONDURAS

3.  I grew up in [[INDIRECT_LOCATION|Colonia Rivera Hernández in San Pedro Sula]], which is controlled by the Mara Salvatrucha
    (MS-13). My mother, [[PERSON|Dilcia Cruz Martínez]], sold tortillas from our house. My father left when I was small.
    I studied until ninth grade at [[SCHOOL|Instituto José Trinidad Reyes]].

4.  In 2016 I began a relationship with [[PERSON|Josué Eduardo Mejía Flores]], who drove a bus on the route 7 line. The
    gang charged him "renta" (extortion) of [[MONEY?|500 lempiras]] a week. In [[DATE?|February 2018]] he could not pay. On
    [[DATE|March 3, 2018]], two gang members shot him at the bus terminal. He died at [[ORGANIZATION?|Hospital Mario Catarino Rivas]].
    I was four months pregnant with [[PERSON MINOR|Kevin]].

5.  After [[PERSON|Josué]]'s death, a gang member known as "[[PERSON|El Flaco]]" -- whose real name I later learned is [[PERSON|Wilmer
    Antonio Reyes Paz]] -- came to my mother's house and said [[PERSON|Josué]] still owed them, and now I owed them. He has a
    [[PHYSICAL_DESCRIPTOR|large "MS" tattoo across his throat and a scar through his left eyebrow]].

6.  On [[DATE|November 21, 2018]], [[PERSON|El Flaco]] and two other men forced me into a car and took me to a house in [[INDIRECT_LOCATION|the
    sector behind the Chamelecón market]]. [[SEXUAL_REPRODUCTIVE_HEALTH|They raped me]]. [[PERSON|El Flaco]] said they did it because I was
    "una negra" and that Garifuna women belong to them. They took my passport and my identity card and said if I
    went to the police they would kill my mother and my baby. I did not go to the police because the police in
    our colonia work with the gang. A neighbor took me to a clinic run by [[ORGANIZATION?|Médicos Sin Fronteras]], where I
    received [[SEXUAL_REPRODUCTIVE_HEALTH|emergency contraception and HIV prevention medicine]]. I have their paper (Exhibit F).
