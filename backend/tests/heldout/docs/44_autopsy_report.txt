OPINION
  This [[AGE|57]]-year-old man died of blunt force injuries sustained when he was struck by a motor vehicle while
  walking in a marked crosswalk. The injury pattern (right-sided bumper fractures at 38-41 cm, right flank
  patterned abrasion, right-sided head impact) is consistent with a pedestrian struck on his right side by
  a high-profile vehicle traveling at moderate to high speed. His therapeutic anticoagulation (apixaban)
  likely accelerated hemorrhage but the head, chest, liver and pelvic injuries were each independently
  potentially fatal. His [[MEDICAL_CONDITION?|hypertensive and atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease]] did not cause or contribute
  to his death. The manner of death is classified as ACCIDENT under this office's conventions for vehicular
  deaths; this classification does not address criminal responsibility, which is determined by law
  enforcement and the courts (see Tex. Penal Code § 49.08).
