         AGREED JUDGMENT AND ORDER APPROVING SETTLEMENT OF MINOR'S CLAIM, APPROVING
       GUARDIAN AD LITEM FEE, DIRECTING DEPOSIT INTO REGISTRY, AND PARTIALLY SEALING RECORD

     On [[DATE|October 9, 2025]], the Court heard the application of [[PERSON|Maria Elena Delgado-Ruiz]], as next
friend of [[PERSON MINOR|M.A.R.]], a minor, for approval of a settlement of the minor's claims against
Defendants. Plaintiff appeared in person and through counsel, [[PERSON|Rachel Kim]]. Defendants appeared
through counsel, [[PERSON|Courtney Ashford]]. The Court previously appointed [[PERSON|Lucinda Paz]] (State Bar No.
[[ID_NUMBER|24070991]]) as guardian ad litem for the minor under Tex. R. Civ. P. 173, and she appeared and
recommended approval.

     The Court, having heard the evidence and the recommendation of the guardian ad litem, FINDS
that the settlement is fair, reasonable, and in the best interest of the minor; that the minor
sustained a [[MEDICAL_CONDITION|forehead laceration, abdominal wall contusion, and acute stress reaction]] for which
he continues in [[MENTAL_HEALTH|trauma-focused play therapy]]; and that the minor has been seen by the Court
in chambers, in the presence of the guardian ad litem.

     IT IS THEREFORE ORDERED that the settlement of [[MONEY?|$65,000.00]] is APPROVED and shall be
disbursed as follows:

     Gross settlement ................................................... [[MONEY?|$65,000.00]]
     Attorney's fees ([[ORGANIZATION?|Kim & Abernathy PLLC]], 25% per Court reduction) ......... [[MONEY?|($16,250.00)]]
     Case expenses allocable to minor's claim ........................... [[MONEY?|($1,140.00)]]
     Guardian ad litem fee ([[PERSON|Lucinda Paz]]), taxed as costs against Defendants ... paid separately
     Medical expenses of minor (to [[ORGANIZATION?|BCBSTX]] ERISA lien, reduced) ................. [[MONEY?|($2,610.00)]]
     Outstanding therapy balance, [[ORGANIZATION?|Little Oaks Child Therapy]] (Dr. [[PERSON|I. Chukwu]]) ...... [[MONEY?|($1,215.00)]]
     NET TO MINOR ........................................................ [[MONEY?|$43,785.00]]

     IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that the net sum of [[MONEY?|$43,785.00]] shall be deposited into the registry of
the Court, pursuant to Tex. Prop. Code § 142.001 and Tex. Loc. Gov't Code § 117.052, and invested
by the District Clerk in an interest-bearing, federally insured account or the Texas Treasury
Safekeeping Trust Company TexPool program, for the benefit of the minor, until the minor attains
the age of eighteen years on [[DATE|October 8, 2036]], at which time the funds, with accrued interest
and less statutory fees, shall be paid to the minor upon proper identification, without further
order. No withdrawal shall be made before that date except upon written motion and order of this
Court upon a showing of necessity.

     IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that the minor's full name, date of birth, Social Security number, and
residence address set out in Exhibit A are SEALED and shall be maintained by the District Clerk in
a sealed envelope, accessible only to the parties, counsel, the guardian ad litem, the District
Clerk for registry purposes, and upon further order of the Court. The Court finds that the minor's
specific, serious, and substantial interest in privacy clearly outweighs the presumption of
openness as to these identifiers only. Tex. R. Civ. P. 76a(1)(a). The remainder of this order is
not sealed.

     Upon funding, Plaintiff's claims individually and as next friend are DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE.
Costs taxed against the party incurring same, except the guardian ad litem fee.
