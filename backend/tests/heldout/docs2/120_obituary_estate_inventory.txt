                         [[PERSON|JESSE LEE HUMMINGBIRD SR.]]
                    [[DATE_OF_BIRTH|November 14, 1962]] -- [[DATE|February 26, 2026]]

[[PERSON|Jesse Lee Hummingbird Sr.]], [[AGE|63]], of [[LOCATION?|Park Hill]], went to be with the Lord on Thursday, [[DATE|February
26, 2026]], from injuries he suffered when his pickup was struck by a gravel truck on Highway 62 near
[[INDIRECT_LOCATION|the Illinois River bridge at Chewey Road]]. He was born in [[LOCATION?|Hulbert]] to [[PERSON|Wilson]] and [[PERSON|Ella (Soap)
Hummingbird]] and was a proud citizen of the Cherokee Nation.

"[[PERSON|J.L.]]," as everyone knew him, graduated from [[SCHOOL|Sequoyah High School]] in 1981 and served four years
in the U.S. Army. For 29 years he was [[INDIRECT_ROLE|the feed-mill foreman at Illinois River Feed & Supply]] ([[EMPLOYER|Illinois
River Feed & Supply]]), and after retiring he drove a school bus for [[EMPLOYER|Keys Public Schools]], where the kids
called him "Mr. [[PERSON|Bird]]." He was a deacon at [[INDIRECT_LOCATION|Fourkiller Indian Baptist Church on Bald Hill Road]], sang
in the Cherokee hymn choir, and never missed a stickball game at the Cherokee National Holiday. He loved
fishing for crappie at Tenkiller and his [[VEHICLE_DESCRIPTION|blue 1978 Ford F-100 that he restored himself]].

He is survived by his wife of 41 years, [[PERSON|Darlene (Christie) Hummingbird]]; his children, [[PERSON|Jesse Lee
Hummingbird Jr.]] ("[[PERSON|Junior]]") and wife [[PERSON|Tasha]] of Tulsa, [[PERSON|Rayna Hummingbird-Pigeon]] and husband
[[PERSON|Corey Pigeon]] of Stilwell, and [[PERSON|Kimberly "Kimmie" Hummingbird]] of Tahlequah; grandchildren [[PERSON MINOR|Kaden]],
[[PERSON MINOR|Aniyah]], [[PERSON MINOR|Wyatt]], and [[PERSON MINOR|Lily]]; his sister [[PERSON|Mae Sixkiller]]; and many nieces and nephews. He
was preceded in death by his parents and his brother [[PERSON|Roger]].

Services were held [[DATE|March 2, 2026]] at [[INDIRECT_LOCATION?|Fourkiller Indian Baptist Church]], with burial at [[INDIRECT_LOCATION|Hummingbird
family cemetery]]. Arrangements by [[ORGANIZATION?|Green Country Funeral Home]], Tahlequah.
