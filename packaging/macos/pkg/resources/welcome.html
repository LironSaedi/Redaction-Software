Welcome to Obscura Redaction Studio

AI redaction for legal and medical records — documents, photos, audio and video.

  • Private by design. Everything runs on this Mac. Nothing is uploaded, ever.
  • Encrypted. Case files are stored with AES-256 encryption.
  • No account needed when only this computer uses it. An office can share one server.

The installer copies the program into Applications › Obscura. Open it when installation finishes and a short setup assistant will guide you through the rest.

To remove Obscura later, open Uninstall Obscura in the same folder, or use the Uninstall button inside the app.
