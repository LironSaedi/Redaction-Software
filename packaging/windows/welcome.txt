Welcome to Obscura Redaction Studio

AI redaction for legal and medical records: documents, photos, audio and video.

  * Private by design. Everything runs on this computer. Nothing is uploaded, ever.
  * Encrypted. Case files are stored with AES-256 encryption.
  * No account needed when only this computer uses it. An office can share one server.

After installation, open Obscura from the Start menu. A short setup assistant opens in your browser and guides you
through the rest (this computer or office server, where to keep case files, AI models).

To remove Obscura later: Settings > Apps > Obscura Redaction Studio > Uninstall, the "Uninstall Obscura" Start-menu
entry, or the Uninstall button inside the app. You will be asked whether to keep your case files.
